  HOW TO USE (Quick Reference)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ADD NEW PROJECT:

  Step 1: Edit .env
    GOOGLE_SHEETS_PROJECT_4_NAME=NewProject
    GOOGLE_SHEETS_PROJECT_4_ID=spreadsheet_id_here

  Step 2: Refresh & Restart
    python scripts/refresh_from_live_sheets.py
    pkill -f production_mcp_integration
    ./start_buildbridge.sh

  Done! ⚡ 30 seconds total.

DEPLOY TO PRODUCTION:

  Step 1: Copy template
    cp .env.template .env

  Step 2: Add production credentials
    (Edit GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, OPENAI_API_KEY)

  Step 3: Add production projects
    (Add GOOGLE_SHEETS_PROJECT_{N}_NAME and _ID)

  Step 4: Deploy
    See docs/PRODUCTION_DEPLOYMENT.md for complete guide
